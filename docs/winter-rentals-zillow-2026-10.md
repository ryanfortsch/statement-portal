# Zillow Rentals and the winter lease plan

Written 2026-10-16 for Dotti and Ryan, prompted by Susan Nolan's note about 17 Beach
("What is the plan regarding splitting the two units up for winter? Do you think that we
will get some reviews this month? I think they're all from Airbnb?").

Everything here is desk research: the city record, the comps sites, Zillow's own pages and
Helm's code. No owner data was pulled from the database. Where a number depends on a home's
own history, the doc says where to read it (`/revenue`).

## The short version

- **Zillow Rental Manager costs nothing to list on.** A basic listing is free and syndicates
  to Zillow, Trulia and HotPads; the optional premium bump is a one-time $39.99 for 90 days.
  Applications are $35, paid by the applicant, and include credit, background and eviction
  screening. It is a one-month-plus channel, not a nightly one.
- **Gloucester draws the short-term line at 30 days.** A rental of 31+ days is not a
  short-term rental under the ordinance and never counts against the 120-day cap that applies
  to non-owner-occupied homes registered with the state after April 1, 2025. For every home in
  the 2026 cohort that is not grandfathered, a winter lease is not just extra income, it is the
  only legal way to earn from the house between the 120 short-term days.
- **Winter furnished rents on Cape Ann run** about $1,600 to $2,400 for a one-bedroom,
  $2,300 to $3,150 for two, $2,900 to $4,000 for three (waterfront to $4,500), and $3,950 to
  $5,900 for four or more. Our homes are furnished to a higher standard than the typical winter
  rental, so each one is priced near the top of its tier with a floor at the middle.
- **17 Beach, priced separately:** main house (4 bedrooms plus bunk room, 3.5 baths)
  $5,400 a month for November through April, or $5,000 for January through April; guest house
  (1 bedroom, 1.5 baths) $2,500 a month with utilities included. The recommended plan is to
  lease the guest house for the whole winter now, keep the main house on the short-term
  calendar through New Year's, and lease it January 2 to April 30.
- **Susan's reviews question has a mechanical answer.** Statement reviews come from the
  booking platforms through Guesty. Stay Cape Ann direct guests (85% of her September) are
  never asked for a review anywhere, and the two new Airbnb unit listings are off Guesty, so
  their reviews (one five-star each so far) never reach Helm. Both are fixable. A draft reply
  is in section 9.

## 1. The rule that changes the math

Gloucester Code of Ordinances, Chapter 5, Article V (Sections 5-63 to 5-69), effective
April 1, 2025, as amended by the City Council on April 8, 2025. Read from the council record,
the city's guidelines page and the Board of Health regulation, each linked under Sources.

| Rule | What it says |
|---|---|
| Definition | A short-term rental is a unit rented "for a term of 30 days or less." A 31-day or longer rental is outside Article V. |
| Day cap, 5-66(h) | A non-owner-occupied unit may be rented short-term for at most 120 consecutive or nonconsecutive calendar days per year. |
| Grandfather, 5-66(j) | Units holding a valid Massachusetts DOR Certificate of Registration as of April 1, 2025 are exempt from the day cap, the 180-day primary-residence cap and the one-unit rule. The three-year sunset in the original ordinance was deleted on April 8, 2025 (roll call 5 to 2). |
| One unit per operator, 5-66(e) | An operator registering after April 1, 2025 may register one non-primary short-term rental. A management company may list and manage it, but the registration must be in the operator's name. |
| Permit count, 5-66(t) | The 500-unit cap on non-primary short-term rentals stays. The motion to delete it failed 2 to 5. |
| Fees | $300 a year for a non-owner-occupied permit ($100 owner-occupied, $150 primary residence). |
| Rental registration | The Board of Health's Rental Registration regulation (adopted April 10, 2025) requires every owner who rents or leases a dwelling unit to register it annually, short-term or long. It defines a "Seasonal Rental" as a fixed-duration lease over 30 days and under a year. Fines run $500 a day. |

What this means for the fleet:

- A home DOR-registered before April 1, 2025 can keep renting short-term year-round. A home
  registered after that date gets 120 short-term days a year. Our 2026 Gloucester launches
  (17 Beach in April, 84 Thatcher in June, 19 Rackliffe and the Marina in July, 225 Washington
  and 3 Windward in August, 7 Sumac in September) are capped unless the owner held a DOR
  certificate before the cutoff. **Check each home's DOR certificate date.** The management
  contract puts compliance on the owner, but the booking calendar is ours.
- For a capped home, 120 short-term days is roughly Memorial Day to Labor Day plus a few
  shoulder weekends. October at 17 Beach ($18,000 across five stays) spends the same budget as
  July. The clean shape is: spend the 120 days May through October, lease November through April.
- A winter lease is a "seasonal rental" to the Board of Health. The unit still needs its annual
  rental registration, which a permitted short-term rental should already carry. Confirm.
- 17 Beach's guest house is an attached accessory unit of the same dwelling. Whether it needs
  its own short-term permit, and whether two Airbnb listings on one lot trip the one-unit rule
  for a post-cutoff registration, is a question for the city, not for this doc.

Rockport and Beverly have no comparable day cap that I found. Rockport has no curbside trash
collection, which a winter tenant needs to be told (transfer station sticker, town bags).

## 2. Zillow Rentals, what it is and what it costs

Zillow Rental Manager is the landlord side of Zillow, Trulia and HotPads. One listing posts
to all three.

- **Listing:** free to publish. Premium placement is a one-time $39.99 per listing for 90 days
  (older pages still quote $29.99, and the posting page still mentions $9.99 a week for extra
  listings, so confirm the price on the day). The form asks rent, deposit, available date and
  preferred lease duration (fixed-term or month-to-month), amenities, photos and a description.
  Furnished, the term, what utilities include and "no occupancy tax on a month-plus lease" go
  in the description.
- **Shelf life:** a listing stays active 21 days. Zillow asks you to confirm it at day 14 and
  deactivates it at day 21 if nobody answers. Put a weekly check on it.
- **Applications:** $35, paid by the applicant, reusable across listings for 30 days. Includes
  credit (Experian), background and eviction history (Checkr). We set the income threshold.
- **Lease and rent:** Zillow has a Massachusetts lease builder with e-sign, and free rent
  collection by ACH (cards carry a fee to the tenant). Rent can land in the owner's account or
  ours; either way it is a revenue line on the statement.
- **What it is not:** a nightly or weekly channel. Zillow Rentals carries one-month-plus
  leases. Do not point the short-term calendar at it.
- **Leads:** come through Zillow's messaging and the phone number on the listing. Volume is
  high and tire-kickers are many. Answer within hours or the lead goes to the next listing.

**Method**

1. Tier by bedrooms and town, then place the home inside the tier by setting: Rocky Neck,
   Good Harbor, the harbor and the Rockport village carry the premium; downtown and
   East Gloucester sit mid-tier; a 1.5-bath four-bedroom prices like a three.
2. List near the top of the tier. Our homes come furnished to short-term standard, with full
   kitchens, linens, smart locks and someone to call. The typical winter rental is a summer
   house with the owner's furniture.
3. Floor at the tier midpoint. Below the floor the lease stops beating the home's own winter
   short-term earnings on the stronger homes.
4. Term: November 1 to April 30. October 15 start where October is soft. Never past May 15.
   Three-month leases carry a 10% premium.
5. Utilities: tenant-paid, in the tenant's name, where the home is separately metered.
   Included with a monthly cap on the attached units.
6. Decision rule per home: lease when last winter's November to April gross on `/revenue` is
   under about eight times the monthly list rent. Six months of rent with no cleanings, host
   fees or heat on empty nights beats a short-term gross up to that line. The holiday weeks are
   the exception and are handled by the term, not the price.

## 6. Lease terms that manage the Massachusetts risks

A winter lease is a full residential tenancy. The 100-day vacation exemption from the
security deposit law and the 31-day exemption from the lead law do not cover it. The terms
below are what keep a six-month tenant from becoming a seven-month one.

- **Fixed term, hard end, no renewal clause.** End April 30 (May 15 at the latest), written
  reminder at 60 days. A holdover is removed by summary process, which takes months, and a
  May holdover eats the summer. Prefer tenants with a documented exit: a contract end date, a
  closing date, an insurer's placement. Avoid month-to-month entirely.
- **No security deposit.** Take first and last month's rent at signing. A deposit on a
  tenancy over 100 days must sit in a separate interest-bearing Massachusetts account with a
  statement of condition inside ten days, and a mistake carries triple damages. Last month's
  rent still earns interest (5% or the bank rate), paid at move-out; small, but owed.
- **Lead paint.** Homes built before 1978 rented to a household with a child under six must
  be deleaded or hold a Letter of Compliance, and a family cannot be turned away because of
  the child. Know each home's status before it goes up. The exemption form we use for
  short-term stays stops at 31 days.
- **Utilities.** Metered homes: electric, gas and oil in the tenant's name, meter and tank
  readings at move-in and move-out. Attached units: included with a cap (the Rockport comps
  use $300 to $600 a month).
- **Insurance.** Short-term policies cover guests, not tenants. The owner confirms landlord
  coverage for the term; the tenant carries renter's insurance with $100,000 liability, with
  Rising Tide and the owner named as additional interests.
- **Furnished inventory.** A photographed inventory at move-in, normal wear expected, and a
  move-out cleaning fee in the lease ($350 to $600 by size) so the house comes back at
  short-term standard.
- **House rules.** No smoking, no subletting or short-term renting by the tenant, two
  occupants per bedroom, pets by owner consent at $100 a month pet rent (winter tenants with
  dogs are a large share of the pool, so saying yes widens it). Snow on walks and driveway is
  the tenant's at a single-family home, in writing.
- **Registration.** Gloucester Board of Health annual rental registration must be current on
  the unit. Rockport tenants get the transfer-station rules on day one.
- **Broker fee.** Since August 1, 2025 the party who hires a broker pays the fee. Zillow leads
  are direct, so no broker, no fee either way.
- **Occupancy tax.** None on 32+ consecutive nights. Do not collect it, and say so in the
  listing; it is a selling point against Airbnb's monthly price.

## 7. What Rising Tide charges on a lease

The management contract deducts the manager's fee from rental income and binds the owner to
a minimum number of days listed and unblocked for short-term rental. A six-month lease touches
both, so each one needs a one-paragraph addendum the owner signs: the lease months do not
count against minimum availability, and the fee on lease income is X.

Recommendation: the home's standard fee percentage on collected rent. Rising Tide finds and
screens the tenant, papers the lease, holds the inventory, takes the maintenance calls, handles
the move-out and restores the house for May. That is more than a placement, and it keeps the
statement simple: one revenue line, the usual fee line, no cleaning line. Ryan's decision;
settle it before the first listing goes up, because the owner will ask.

For 7 Sumac the signed contract already carries a 5% off-season discount on 30-plus-night
stays January through April. That clause prices short-term monthly stays, not a lease, but it
tells you the Montagninos expected winter month-plus occupants.

## 8. Reviews: why September showed none, and what reaches Susan's statement

Three facts from the code and the live listings:

- The statement's review block reads `reviews`, which `/api/sync-guesty` fills from Guesty.
  Guesty carries Airbnb, VRBO and Booking.com reviews only. It is month-scoped by when the
  review landed, not by the stay.
- Stay Cape Ann direct guests have no platform review to write, and the concierge's
  review-request engine skips them by design (`review_requests.py`: "Direct/manual
  (staycapeann) bookings have no Guesty channel to message through AND no OTA review to
  request, so they're skipped here"). Four of Susan's five September stays were direct. Nobody
  was asked.
- `17_beach_front` and `17_beach_back` are Helm-run and off Guesty, so their Airbnb reviews
  never enter `reviews` and never print on a statement. Each has one five-star review today.
  Airbnb shows a listing's average once it has three.

So Susan is right that the reviews she sees are all from Airbnb, and the reason September
showed none is that her September was almost all direct. October's whole-house Airbnb and
VRBO stays will produce reviews through Guesty as usual; the units' reviews will not appear
until Helm has a way to record them.
