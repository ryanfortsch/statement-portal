## 2. The account, the phone and the inbox

- **One account.** Rising Tide lists everything from the Zillow Rental Manager account already
  signed in on Ryan's Chrome ("Manage rentals"). One account keeps the leads, applications and
  leases in one place and avoids Zillow's one-listing-per-address collisions.
- **The contact phone.** Zillow verifies the number on the listing by text or call before a
  listing goes live, and it says a managed or auto-responder number will not pass and some
  number types are refused. Try the Rising Tide 24/7 line, (978) 865-2500, first; it is the back
  office line and a tenant lead belongs there. If Zillow refuses it, verify Ryan's cell and
  forward. Never the GUESTS line (the concierge would answer tenant leads as guests) and never
  the OWNERS line. A verified number stays verified for every later listing.
- **The inbox.** Leads arrive in Rental Manager's messages and as email to the account. Check it
  at 9, 1 and 5 on weekdays. Premium adds a tracking phone number, which is the only reason to
  pay for premium on a home that is already getting calls.

  applications and leases.

## 3. Building a listing, field by field

Zillow's flow: Add a property, enter the full address, the unit and the property type, then
fill Property info, Listing details, Lease, Media and Amenities (saved as you go), then Final
details (contact, tour availability, whether to accept Zillow applications), then publish free
or premium. Up to 24 hours to show on Zillow, Trulia and HotPads. A listing can be declined;
the usual reasons are an address that does not resolve, photos with text or watermarks, and
copy that reads like a scam.

| Field | What we enter |
|---|---|
| Address and unit | The real street address. Two listings at one address need distinct units: 17 Beach Rd "Main House" and 17 Beach Rd "Guest House" |
| Property type | House for the main houses; Apartment for the guest house and the Marina |
| Beds, baths, square feet | Bedrooms as Airbnb counts them (the bunk room counts), half baths as .5. Square feet from the assessor's card |
| Rent | The list rent. Say "utilities included" or "tenant pays gas and electric" in the first line of the description too |
| Security deposit | $0. The description says "first and last month's rent at signing, no security deposit" |
| Lease term | Fixed term with both dates. Zillow's own renter filter is "Short term lease available"; make sure the listing qualifies for it |
| Available date | Nov 1 or Jan 2 |
| Furnished | Yes, in amenities and in the first sentence |
| Utilities included | Attached units: all. Metered houses: water and sewer, internet; tenant pays gas or oil and electric |
| Pets | What the owner said. "Cats and dogs considered, $100 a month pet rent" widens the pool by about a third |
| Laundry | In unit |
| Parking | Off-street, the count the KB supports |
| Heating and cooling | As the KB says (17 Beach: central plus mini-splits) |
| Amenities | Dishwasher, washer and dryer, air conditioning, high-speed internet, smart lock, outdoor space, grill where present |
| Photos | 25 to 35 from the Airbnb set, in the order below. No text, no logos, no winter-dated shots |
| Description | The template in section 4 |
| Tour availability | By appointment, weekdays and Saturday mornings, between guest stays |
| Zillow applications | On. The renter pays the $35 (Zillow's FAQ) and the reports come to us |
| Contact | Rising Tide STR, the verified phone, the account email |
| Free or premium | Free for wave 1. Upgrade a listing to premium ($39.99, one time, 90 days, not refundable) only if it has no qualified application after 7 days |

Photo order that works on a rental search: exterior front, living room, kitchen, primary
bedroom, primary bath, second bedroom, dining, porch or patio, remaining bedrooms and baths,
laundry, yard, parking, a beach shot last. Airbnb's hero shots are built for a vacation search;
a tenant wants to see the kitchen and the bedrooms early.

## 4. The description template

First sentence carries rent terms and the dates, because Zillow truncates the preview. Plain
sentences, no exclamation marks, no em dashes, no "luxury". State the facts a tenant checks
first: furnished, utilities, term, pets, parking, laundry, internet.

> Furnished [N]-bedroom [house / suite] in [neighborhood], Gloucester, available [date] through
> [date]. [Rent terms sentence: utilities included / tenant pays gas and electric.] First and
> last month's rent at signing, no security deposit. No occupancy tax on a month-plus lease.
>
> [Two or three sentences on the home: layout, kitchen, baths, laundry, outdoor space, parking,
> heating and cooling, internet speed.]
>
> [One sentence on location: the beach, the commuter rail, the hospital, downtown, Boston.]
>

> appointment. Applications through Zillow.

## 5. Pricing discipline

- List at the list rent. Drop to the floor after 14 days without a qualified application,
  never below the floor.
- A three-month lease is the list rent plus 10%. A four-month lease (Jan 2 to Apr 30) is the
  Jan-to-Apr figure in the pricing doc.
- Attached units include utilities with a cap in the lease ($150 a month of electric at the
  guest house and the Marina, overage billed). Metered houses put gas or oil and electric in
  the tenant's name.
- Pet rent $100 a month where the owner allows pets. No pet deposits (a deposit is a deposit
  under Massachusetts law).
- No concessions, no free weeks, no "negotiable" in the copy. The floor is the concession.

## 6. Leads, showings and screening

**Reply within two hours in business hours, same day otherwise.** The first reply asks five
things and nothing else: the dates they need, who is moving in, why the stay ends in April
(contract end, closing, a claim, a renovation), household income, pets. A lead that cannot
name an exit in April is not a winter tenant.

**Showings** are accompanied, 30 minutes, scheduled between guest stays, with the house in
turnover condition. A video walkthrough covers out-of-area applicants (traveling nurses and
insurance placements rarely visit first). No self-showing codes, ever.

**Criteria, written down and applied to everyone the same way:** income of three times the
rent from verifiable sources, credit score 650 or better on Zillow's report, no evictions in
seven years, government ID that matches the application, two references (current landlord and
employer or agency), and a documented reason the tenancy ends in April. Decide within 48 hours.
A denial based on the credit or background report gets a written adverse-action notice naming
the report (federal law).

**Massachusetts fair housing applies in full.** Protected classes include race, color, national
origin, religion, sex, familial status, disability, age, marital status, sexual orientation,
gender identity, veteran status, genetic information and source of income. In practice:

- Never write "no children" or "adults only," and never turn a family away because of a child.
  A household with a child under six triggers the lead law on a pre-1978 home (the pricing doc,
  section 6).
- Treat a housing voucher as income. "No Section 8" is illegal to say and to do.
- Say "no pets" if the owner says so; a service or assistance animal is not a pet.
- Keep every applicant's answers to the same five questions, in writing, so the file shows
  the same test was applied.

## 7. The lease and the move-in

Rental Manager takes an uploaded lease, fills its dynamic fields (names, dates, rent) and
sends it for e-signature, and it stores the signed copy next to the tenant's payments and
messages. Use Rising Tide's own fixed-term Massachusetts lease that way rather than Zillow's
state templates. The clauses are the ones in the pricing doc, section 6:

- fixed term, hard end date, no renewal or holdover clause, 60-day reminder;
- first and last month's rent at signing, no security deposit, interest on the last month
  paid at move-out;
- utilities as listed, meter and tank readings at move-in and move-out;
- furnished inventory with photographs attached as an exhibit, normal wear expected,
  move-out cleaning fee ($350 for the guest house and the Marina, $600 for the main houses);
- renter's insurance with $100,000 liability, Rising Tide and the owner named;
- no smoking, no subletting or short-term renting, two occupants per bedroom, pets as agreed;
- snow and ice on walks and driveway are the tenant's at a single-family home;
- Gloucester's cart rule in the lease: carts go out the night before pickup and come back in
  once they're emptied ($400 per occurrence if not).

Collect first and last through Zillow Payments (free by ACH) into the property's own Chase
account, so the deposit shows on the bank CSV the statement already reads.

Move-in day: inventory walk and photographs, meter and tank readings, a time-boxed Seam
access code for the lease dates (never the guest code), the trash-day card on the fridge, the
house guide, the 24/7 line as the emergency number, and the tenant's renter's insurance
certificate in the file.

## 8. What changes in Helm the day a lease signs

1. **Block the short-term calendars** for the lease dates: a manual block on the Guesty
   listing with reason "Winter lease"; for the 17 Beach units, the block on the whole house
   and the unit, since `listing_links` keeps the three from double-selling; the Stay Cape Ann
   calendar follows Guesty.
2. **Set a rental period** on the property page for the lease months
   (`property_rental_periods`), so the forecast and revenue pacing stop projecting
   short-term nights.
3. **Pause guest automations** for the home (`automations_enabled` on the property), so the
   concierge, trash reminders and review asks do not fire at a tenant.
4. **Cleaner schedule:** no turnovers in the window; one deep clean after move-out.
5. **Seam:** a tenant access code for the lease dates; the guest and cleaner codes stay in
   place for the spring reset.
6. **Statement:** rent lands in the property's Chase account by ACH. On the bank CSV it is an
   unmatched non-Stripe deposit, which the operator attributes to the property-month in the
   deposit-review queue. An attributed deposit is add-on revenue and enters the fee base, so
   the management fee applies without any new code. No cleaning line, no tax remittance. The
   fee percentage on lease income is Ryan's call (pricing doc, section 7) and the owner
   addendum states it.
7. **The owner's file:** the lease, the inventory, the insurance certificate and the addendum
   go in the property's Documents tab and the contracts Drive folder.

## 11. Stop rules

- No lease ending after May 15. No month-to-month. No security deposits.
- No applicant without a documented April exit, whatever the income.
- A lease signed at a home still open for short-term bookings is blocked in Helm the same
  day, before the tenant has keys.
- No listing goes live without the owner's written yes and the addendum.
- The copy never promises a view, a parking count or an amenity the KB does not support.
